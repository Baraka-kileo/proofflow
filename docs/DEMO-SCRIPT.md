# Four-minute demo script

## 0:00–0:30 — The problem

“This SME completed a real order, but payment is due in 60 days. The business needs working capital now. Funders can help, but proving the invoice is genuine is slow and fragmented.”

## 0:30–1:30 — SME creates proof

Open the SME dashboard, start/continue the seeded application, and upload the three synthetic documents. Show extraction progress, correct one flagged field, and run verification. Open one passed rule and one review warning to demonstrate explainability.

## 1:30–2:15 — Buyer confirms

Switch to the buyer account in a private browser window. Open the pending request, preview delivery evidence, check the attestations, and confirm. Return to the SME view and show the live status/timeline change.

## 2:15–3:10 — Funder offers

Switch to the funder account. Open the now buyer-confirmed package, show duplicate checks and the audit trail, then create a simulated offer. State clearly that a real lender would apply its regulated credit process here.

## 3:10–3:40 — SME accepts

Return to the SME account, explain invoice value, fee, and net advance, then accept the simulated offer. Show the Trust Passport update.

## 3:40–4:00 — Why it wins

“ProofFlow does not promise magic money. It removes a real bottleneck: trustworthy, reusable evidence. It is accessible on mobile, interoperable through structured data and APIs, protected by tenant isolation and private storage, and has a clear funder-paid business model.”

## Demo fallback

Keep seeded extracted data and all role sessions ready. If Gemini is unavailable, use the UI's explicitly labelled demo extraction fallback; never pretend it was a live model response.
