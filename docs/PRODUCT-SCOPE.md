# Product scope

ProofFlow helps an SME prove that completed work supports a real, outstanding invoice. It organizes the evidence and confirmation a funder needs to review; the funder still owns identity checks, credit judgement, pricing, contracts and disbursement.

## Included in the working MVP

- private upload of a purchase order, delivery record and invoice;
- manual entry of 21 facts linked to those source documents;
- 12 transparent checks comparing names, references, currency, amounts, arithmetic, dates, delivery acknowledgement and possible duplicates;
- a clear pass, review or fail result for each check, including the values compared;
- authorised customer-system lookup only when a real connection exists;
- authenticated large-customer confirmation or dispute;
- immutable confirmation receipt and downloadable PDF certificate;
- funding-ready evidence package and Trust Passport history;
- external KYC/KYB progress recorded by the funding partner without copying raw identity evidence;
- independent funding proposal or decline;
- server authorization, database tenant isolation, private storage, signed links and audit events.

## Deliberately outside the MVP

- AI reading or extracting uploaded financial documents;
- automated KYC/KYB, credit scoring or complete underwriting;
- lending, custody of funds, rate setting or repayment guarantees;
- storage of biometrics, ID-document images, sanctions reports or confidential underwriting reasons;
- fabricated SAP, Coupa, bank, compliance or disbursement responses;
- claims of certification, bank partnership or production regulatory approval.

The fictional hosted records and [sample documents for testing](../samples/sample-documents-for-testing/README.md) use the same workflow as ordinary records. They are evidence that the software works, not evidence of a real external transaction.
