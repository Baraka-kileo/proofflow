# Product scope

ProofFlow helps an SME turn completed work into a funding-ready evidence package. It structures evidence and makes verification traceable; it does not replace a funder's compliance, credit or legal responsibilities.

## Included

- Private purchase-order, delivery-evidence and invoice upload
- Manual, atomic evidence entry with an SME declaration
- Deterministic V001–V012 consistency and duplicate checks
- Authorised customer-system lookup when a real connection exists
- Authenticated large-customer confirmation or dispute
- Funding-ready package and Trust Passport history
- External compliance workflow status recorded by a funding partner
- Independent funding proposals and decisions
- Role isolation, private storage, signed URLs and immutable audit events

## Excluded

- AI, document extraction, confidence scores or model responses
- KYC/KYB decisions, credit scores or complete underwriting
- Lending, custody of funds, rate setting or repayment guarantees
- Fabricated SAP, Coupa, bank, KYC or disbursement responses

Fictional seed records populate this same product model for development and evaluation.
